address of next instruction = address of current instruction + length of current instruction in bytes;

near jump in x86:
byte representation of destination address = destination address - address from where to jump - 5 (length of near jump instruction in bytes);

near jump instruction in bytes: [E9 byte_representation_of_destination_address]
near jump instruction in assembly language: [jmp destination_address]
